## My opinion first

I think **indexes are the best starting point** for learning markets. One number tells you the mood of 30–50 big companies. I check the *level + valuation*, not just "up or down".

> Educational only. Not financial advice. Not a recommendation on any fund or stock.

## What is an index?

An index = a **basket of top companies**, weighted mostly by size (free-float market cap).

- **Nifty 50** → 50 large companies on NSE
- **Sensex** → 30 large companies on BSE

If the basket's total value rises, the index rises.

## How I read it

| What I check | Why |
|---|---|
| Index level | Mood — where are we vs 1 year ago? |
| PE (price-to-earnings) | Are we paying more per rupee of profit? |
| Breadth | Are few stocks pulling, or many rising? |

High PE does not mean "sell tomorrow". It means **be humble, expect lower future returns, manage risk**.

## Beginner trap I see

Buying whatever is trending because the index is at a high. An index high is **not** a buy signal by itself.
