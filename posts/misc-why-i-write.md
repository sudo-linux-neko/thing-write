## Anything goes here

Markets are the main dish. **Misc is the notebook.**

I will post:

- book notes
- small tech experiments (like this file-based blog!)
- life notes

## How this blog works (meta)

Each post is a **file** in `/posts`. I can write in the browser (Write ✎ button) and import `.txt`. Then I export `.md` and drop it here. No database.

If you're reading this on Netlify — hi! Star the repo if you like file-based things.
