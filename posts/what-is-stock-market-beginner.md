## My short opinion

The stock market is **not a money machine**. It is a place where parts of real businesses change hands. If you treat it like learning, it teaches you. If you treat it like a lottery, it bills you.

> This post is educational only — not financial advice. Not a buy / sell / hold recommendation.

## The basics in 2 minutes

- A **share** = tiny ownership in a company.
- An **exchange** (NSE / BSE in India) = the marketplace where buyers and sellers meet.
- **Price** = last agreed price between a buyer and seller. It moves on expectations, earnings, interest rates, and mood.

## Why prices move (my mental model)

1. **Earnings** — is the business making more money?
2. **Interest rates** — high rates make safe options attractive, pressuring stocks.
3. **Flow + mood** — short term, fear and greed dominate.

I think beginners should watch **index + earnings** first, tips last.

## How I would start learning (not advice)

- Read one index page (Nifty 50) for 10 minutes a week.
- Learn one ratio at a time: PE, then ROE, then debt.
- Paper-track 3 companies you actually understand.
