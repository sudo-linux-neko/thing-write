## The opinion

**Returns are loud. Risk is quiet — until it isn't.** In my view, most beginner damage comes from:

1. Too-big position size
2. No emergency fund, so every dip feels existential
3. Leverage / F&O before understanding spot

> Not advice. Just how I think. Talk to a licensed professional for your situation.

## My 3 rules for learning safely

- **Never invest money you need in 3 years** into stocks while learning.
- **One idea = small size.** If you're excited, that's a signal to go *smaller*.
- **Write the reason before buying** (even on paper). If you can't write 3 lines, skip.
